# SemID vs CM4DI — Preliminary Comparison

Owner: #102 / #136  
Status: **Preliminary; full-text ontology extraction remains required.**

SemID is a historical semantic-web ontology for corporate identity management and access to project resources. It is material because it establishes that ontology-based IAM predates CM4DI.

Current comparison:
- SemID is enterprise/corporate IAM-centered; CM4DI is cross-paradigm.
- SemID's role/policy/resource vocabulary must be compared with CM4DI's separation of contextual UFO roles from IAM `PermissionBundle`, `Permission`, `AccessPolicy`, `AccessGrant`, `Principal`, `Resource` and `Action`.
- CM4DI adds explicit foundational grounding, DDD ownership, credential/wallet, government/trust, workload/device/agent identity, standards mappings and reproducible evaluation requirements.
- No claim that CM4DI is the first identity ontology is permitted.
