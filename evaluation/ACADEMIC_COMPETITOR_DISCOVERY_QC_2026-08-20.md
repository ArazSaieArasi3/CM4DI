## Major findings
1. The journal extension cannot rely on novelty claims based merely on “ontology,” “UFO,” “SSI interoperability,” or “broad digital-identity conceptualization”; prior work exists for each of those individually.
2. The defensible CM4DI contribution is the combination of **cross-paradigm scope + foundational grounding + Core/Profile modularity + explicit standards/government/IAM/non-human mappings + executable formalization + reproducible multi-layer evaluation**.
3. MFSSIA is a serious direct comparator but is specialized around blockchain-based challenge-set SSI authentication in M2X, whereas CM4DI’s intended mission is cross-paradigm semantic interoperability.
4. Comb & Martin 2026 is especially important because it already performs large-scale ontology-oriented synthesis and explicitly proposes ontological fragments; CM4DI should cite it as motivation/precursor and differentiate through formal engineering and evaluation.
5. Recent SSI literature strongly supports keeping governance/principles separate from implementation artifacts and avoiding an SSI-centric Core.
6. Credential-exchange roles such as issuer/holder/verifier are contextual interaction roles and require UFO role analysis rather than rigid actor taxonomy.
7. Identity/account/profile, identity/credential lifecycle, trust/governance, context and evidence/proof distinctions converge with prior normative and operational findings.

## Residual limitations
- The bounded corpus is curated, not an exhaustive PRISMA/SLR corpus.
- Some relevant industry conceptual frameworks (e.g., ToIP) are retained only as secondary/background triangulation and must not be counted as peer-reviewed evidence.
- The Nahar IAM ontology/metamodel source is a thesis; it is useful for competitor awareness but should not be given the same evidential weight as peer-reviewed journal work.
- AI-agent academic literature remains immature; current agent evidence is primarily operational and will remain profile-first unless later academic/standards convergence strengthens it.
- Final novelty claims must be rechecked immediately before journal submission because the field is moving rapidly.
