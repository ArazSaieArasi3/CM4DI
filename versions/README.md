# CM4DI Version Navigation

CM4DI has a published conference baseline and an active journal-extension research track. The two must remain easy to distinguish without breaking historical artifact paths.

| Line | Purpose | Status | Starting point |
|---|---|---|---|
| **v1 / PUB-002** | Published ICWR 2026 conference baseline | Published and immutable as scholarly lineage | [`v1-published/README.md`](v1-published/README.md) |
| **v2 / PUB-003** | Substantial journal extension and reference-ontology program | Active research | [`v2-journal/README.md`](v2-journal/README.md) |

## Compatibility rule

The original root artifacts such as `CM4DI.owl`, `CM4DI-Generation2-Version15.drawio`, `CM4DI-Generation2-Version15.jpg`, `CM4DI-field-registry-v0.1.md` and `case-study-pharmaceutical-ecosystem.md` remain at their established paths. Version navigation adds stable reader guidance; it does not relocate or silently reinterpret the published baseline.

## Semantic authority

The v1 artifacts are authoritative only for the published conference baseline. Current journal-v2 semantics are governed by the Gate-C concept/relation registries, DDD artifacts, profiles, mappings and later formal-ontology artifacts after their gates pass.
