## Non-delta items
The journal extension does **not** claim novelty merely from adding more classes, more pages, more references or another diagram. Existing conference semantics are treated as historical baseline and changed only through governed semantic decisions.

## Current status
The delta architecture is scientifically substantial in design, but several rows remain **planned rather than evidenced** until Source Completeness, formalization and Wave-8 evaluation complete. PUB-003 must report actual completed deltas, not the intended roadmap.
